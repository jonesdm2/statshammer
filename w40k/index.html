Where the data comes from

Every unit comes from data/w40k-units.csv in the Statshammer GitHub repository, built from the BSData 11th edition catalogues. The lab reads that file and works out all the probabilities itself.

Left out, or worth knowing about
Method and limits
  • Rules used (11th edition Core Rules, checked 15 Sep 2026):
    • An unmodified 1 always fails and an unmodified 6 is a critical hit or wound. Hit and wound modifiers are capped at ±1.
    • The save is the better of armour with the attack's AP and any invulnerable save; an unmodified 1 always fails.
    • Damage goes to one model at a time and never carries over, so the last model wastes what it can't take. Feel No Pain is rolled for each lost wound.
    • Lethal Hits auto-wound (and are not critical wounds), Sustained Hits add normal hits, Devastating Wounds end the sequence with mortal wounds. Benefit of cover worsens a shooting attack's Ballistic Skill by 1.
    • Units with different models (a sergeant, a bodyguard, a character) use one wound pool, and CHARACTER models are allocated last.
  • Each unit uses its default loadout at its base size. Detachment rules, army rules, stratagems, auras, leaders attached to units and re-rolls from abilities are not included: this is the datasheet on its own.
  • Weapons with several firing modes (marked ➤) are read on one profile. A variable ability such as Sustained Hits D3 is read at its average, and an ability that only works against some targets, such as Lethal Hits against non-vehicles, is left out. Both kinds are listed in the panel above.
  • Feel No Pain is only counted where a datasheet gives it without conditions, which is two units. Most Feel No Pain in the game comes from abilities and army rules.
  • Units over 1,000 points are left out so Apocalypse-scale models don't distort a matched-play reference.
  • Twin-linked, Anti-X, Blast, Torrent, Melta, Rapid Fire, Heavy and Lance follow the 10th edition wording; they have not yet been re-read in the 11th edition text.
  • Because of the missing army rules, factions that lean on them — T'au and the Knights especially — sit lower on the matrix than they play.
