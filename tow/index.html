Both units strike in the same round and every blow lands, as if they fought at the same Initiative. Casualties aren't removed before the other side strikes, and charge bonuses, Hatred and Frenzy aren't included.

The break testRoll 2D6 against Leadership after losing a round

If the natural roll beats your Leadership, you break and flee. If it doesn't but roll plus the margin does, you fall back in good order. Otherwise you give ground. A double one always gives ground. If the winners have more than double your Unit Strength, falling back becomes fleeing. A Battle Standard lets you re-roll a roll that breaks.

Where the data comes from

Every unit comes from data/tow-units.csv in the Statshammer GitHub repository. It was built from Old World Builder (points, unit sizes, equipment) joined to the Online Rules Index (profiles, weapons and rules). The lab reads that file and works out all the probabilities itself.

Method and limits
  • Rules from the Online Rules Index (15 Sep 2026):
    • To hit uses the Weapon Skill chart; a natural 1 always fails and a natural 6 always hits. To wound uses the Strength against Toughness chart.
    • Armour is rolled with AP as a modifier, then one ward, then Regeneration. The best armour is 2+ for infantry and cavalry, 3+ for chariots, monsters and war machines. A shield gives +1, but not with a weapon that Requires Two Hands.
    • Armour Bane improves AP on a natural 6 to wound. Cleaving Blow ignores armour and Regeneration on a natural 6 against infantry, cavalry and war beasts. Poisoned Attacks give +2 to wound on a natural 6 to hit.
  • Only weapons with Fight in Extra Rank make supporting attacks, one per supporting model. Rank bonus depends on troop type (Horde +1). Combat result counts unsaved wounds, capped at the enemy's total Wounds, plus ranks, standard, Battle Standard, flank or rear.
  • Break test: Stubborn declines its first test. Unbreakable units don't test. Unstable units lose a Wound per point they lose by, less Indomitable.
  • Not modelled: charge bonuses (Impact Hits, lances), Hatred, Frenzy, Strike First/Last and casualty order, magic items, spells, shooting, characters joined to units, and Killing Blow / Multiple Wounds. Each unit uses its default weapon and mount.
  • Assumptions pending a rules check: wounds carry over between rank-and-file models, and a ward and Regeneration can both be rolled against the same wound.
